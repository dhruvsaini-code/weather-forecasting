DELHI

Thirty years of daily temperature, forecast a full year ahead to within 2.1 °C.

A time-series study of Delhi's weather from 1 January 1996 to 18 March 2026. Forecasts are tested on 365 unseen days, 19 March 2025 to 18 March 2026.
Phase 2 summary

Seven models

Average error (RMSE, °C) over the 365 test days. Shorter bar means a better forecast.

How we chose

Each step follows from the one before it.

1

Read the seasons

Phase 1 found that the yearly cycle dominates Delhi's temperature (seasonal strength 0.936) while the long-term trend is weak (0.082).

2

Damp the trend

Holt-Winters with a damping factor stops the forecast from drifting upward forever, which is why it beats the undamped version.

3

Check the errors

Residuals average near zero, look like noise, and pass the Ljung-Box test (p above 0.30), so little structure is left unexplained.

Who it helps

Power grid

Summer demand in Delhi passes 8,000 MW. A summer error near 1.9 °C lets planners reserve capacity ahead of cooling-heavy days.

Public health

On days of 38 °C and above, the model stays within roughly 1.7 °C, enough to support early heatwave advisories.

Winter planning

On cold days at or below 8 °C, error is about 2 °C, useful for scheduling night shelters and anticipating smog trapping.
